# Conversation Path: design (draft for Rob's approval)

Date: 2026-10-18 · Status: proposed · Owner: Rob

## Problem

The 93 lesson units are organised as a picture dictionary. A1 teaches colours, animals, fruit, body parts and
classroom objects (chalk) before pronouns, the present tense and question words, which only arrive in A2. Core
conversational words are missing from the course altogether: *i, ali, jer, ako, kad, ovo, to, ovde, tamo, sad,
već, još, uvek, često, sve, mnogo, malo, vreme, dan, čovek, ljudi, stvar, život, problem*. A learner can name a
cucumber but cannot say "I don't have time" or "I'll call you tomorrow".

Part 1 (shipped separately) fixes the flashcard order. This document plans part 2: a lesson path built from the
conversations people actually have.

## Principles

1. **Start from a situation, not a topic.** Each step is a conversation the learner will really have ("ordering a
   coffee", "saying what you do for work"), with a clear can-do goal.
2. **Phrases before grammar names.** Useful chunks come first (*Treba mi…*, *Možete li da…?*); the grammar behind
   them is explained once the learner already uses it.
3. **Core words first, nouns only as needed.** Each step adds the verbs, question words and connectors it needs and
   only the nouns that situation uses. Specialist vocabulary stays in Topic packs.
4. **Recycle.** Every step reuses words from earlier steps in new sentences, so the pool keeps growing without
   dropping old words.
5. **Real Serbian.** Ekavian, Latin script, standard forms, with common colloquial forms (*ćao, može, ajde*) taught
   and labelled as colloquial. Every sentence is independently reviewed before it ships.

## The path

Four stages, about 30 steps. Each step: a short dialogue (6–10 lines), 5–8 key phrases, 6–12 words (new or
recycled), one grammar note, then exercises.

## What happens to the existing 93 units

Nothing is deleted. Unit and word ids are permanent (see CLAUDE.md), so the 93 units become **Topic packs**: a
browsable list (Food, Animals, Classroom, Politics…) under the path, always open, for vocabulary on a subject the
learner cares about. Progress in them stays exactly as it is.

## How it fits the app (technical)

- **Path steps are new units** with new ids (94 onward), so existing ids and progress are untouched.
- **No duplicate cards.** A step lists the existing words it reuses by word id (`r:[...]`) plus its own new words
  (`w:[...]`, only words not already in the course). `buildUnits` resolves references after all units are built, so
  a reused word is the same flashcard everywhere. New words get ids `unit.id*100+position` as usual.
- **Lesson engine:** steps use the existing exercise types (multiple choice, matching, typing, word bank) plus a
  dialogue screen built on the Reading engine (tap-to-translate) and step-specific cloze items for the grammar note.
- **Lessons tab:** shows the path (stages and steps) as the main map; Topic packs below it.
- **Flashcards:** `FC_NEW_ORDER_SR` becomes path order first (step 1's words, then step 2's…), then everything else
  by the part 1 usefulness tiers.
- **Starting point for existing learners:** a step opens when the previous one is done, or straight away when the
  learner already knows at least 80% of its words. A "test out" quiz lets anyone skip ahead.
- **Man/woman forms:** past tense and some adjectives depend on the speaker (*bio sam / bila sam*, *umoran /
  umorna*). A Profile setting ("I speak as a man / a woman / show both") picks which forms exercises expect. New
  `st` field, added to defaults, `getStateData()` and `applyStateData()`.

## Content quality

Each stage is written, then reviewed by an independent reviewer against the CLAUDE.md checklist (wrong options that
are also correct, clitic order, aspect, plural accusatives, ekavian). Judgment calls (standard vs colloquial, word
choice) are listed for Rob instead of being decided silently.

## Build order
