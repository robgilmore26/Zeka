What is Zeka?

A free, ad-free app for learning Serbian (and Italian) from A1 to C1. It combines short topical lessons, spaced-repetition flashcards, and drills for the parts of Serbian that trip most learners up: noun cases, verb conjugation and aspect, and real sentences.

Open Zeka

Guest vs. account

Try without an account puts you straight into the app. Your progress is saved in this browser on this device — close the tab and come back, and it's still there. It won't follow you to another device, and clearing site data removes it.

Signing in (Google or email) keeps the same learning, and adds cloud sync across devices, a username and friend code, and a friends list. Signing in later from a guest session starts from your account's progress, not the guest's.

How learning works

  • Lessons unlock in order. Each unit introduces about ten words, then tests them. Tap a locked unit to see what to finish first.
  • Flashcards use spaced repetition: after you grade a card (Again / Hard / Good / Easy) it comes back in minutes, then a day, then longer, as your memory strengthens. Every card shows the word in a real sentence.
  • Due cards are scheduled for review now. New cards are words you haven't met yet, introduced in course order. The daily limit caps new words per day so tomorrow's reviews stay manageable — you can raise it on the flashcard screen, and "Keep going" lets you push past it.
  • Match, Verbs, Cases and Sentences are extra practice: cross-topic matching, present-tense conjugation, all seven cases, and a daily sentence with blanks.
  • Streak counts consecutive days with any completed session. ELO rises and falls with lesson accuracy.

Typing Serbian

Answers are checked without regard to case or diacritics, so obecavati is accepted for obećavati. A row of š ž č ć đ keys appears under text fields if you want to type them properly.

Languages

Serbian has the full course: 90+ units, 80 conjugated verbs, case drills and a sentence bank. Italian has the lesson units and flashcards; verb, case and sentence drills aren't available for it yet. Switch languages from the flag in the top-left of the Learn screen — progress is kept separately per language.
